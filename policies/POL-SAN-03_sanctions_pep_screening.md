# POL-SAN-03 — Sanctions, PEP and Adverse Media Screening

> MOCK internal policy for demonstration.

## 1. Scope of screening
The company, its directors, shareholders, beneficial owners and its main transaction counterparties must be screened against sanctions lists (including the UN Security Council consolidated list), the national list, the bank's internal blacklist and the PEP database.

## 2. Sanctions matches
A confirmed or probable match with a sanctions list results in an immediate block of the relationship and of any pending transaction, and mandatory escalation to Compliance. Assets must be frozen where required by law. No credit or onboarding decision can be made until Compliance has cleared the match.

## 3. Potential matches and false positives
Name similarity alone does not confirm a match. Potential matches must be reviewed by an analyst who compares date of birth, nationality, address and identifiers. The review and its conclusion must be documented in the file.

## 4. Politically exposed persons (PEP)
A director, shareholder or beneficial owner who is, or was within the last 5 years, a PEP triggers enhanced due diligence: source of wealth and source of funds verification and senior management approval of the relationship.

## 5. Internal blacklist
Any match with the internal blacklist requires Compliance review before any new product or credit is granted.

## 6. Adverse media
Negative press related to fraud, corruption, money laundering or consumer complaints must be documented and assessed for reputational risk.
