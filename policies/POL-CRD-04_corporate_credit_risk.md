# POL-CRD-04 — Corporate Credit Risk Appetite

> MOCK internal policy for demonstration. Ratios are illustrative.

## 1. Leverage
The debt-to-equity ratio (total liabilities / equity) should not exceed 3.0. Above 3.0 the file requires additional guarantees or a reduced exposure.

## 2. Liquidity
The current ratio (current assets / current liabilities) should be at least 1.0. A ratio below 1.0 indicates working capital pressure.

## 3. Debt service capacity
The debt service coverage ratio (EBITDA / annual debt service) should be at least 1.5. Between 1.0 and 1.5 the file is acceptable only with strong guarantees. Below 1.0 the request is outside risk appetite.

## 4. Profitability and growth
Negative net income or negative equity requires a recovery plan. Revenue growth above 100% in one year is unusual and must be explained and supported by contracts or audited accounts.

## 5. Financial history
At least two years of financial statements are required for credit facilities above 5,000,000 MAD. Exceptions require Credit Committee approval.

## 6. Exposure relative to size
Requested exposure above 50% of annual revenue requires Credit Committee approval.
